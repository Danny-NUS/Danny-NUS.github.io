About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Computing, National University of Singapore, advised by Prof. Ye Wang in the Sound and Music Computing Lab. My research studies generative models for speech, singing voice, music, and human-centered multimodal signals.

Before my Ph.D., I received my M.Sc. in Computer Science from NUS, where my thesis focused on singing voice synthesis with technique control. I received my B.Sc. degree from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications and Queen Mary University of London, where I worked on symbolic music generation.

I also have a long musical background in piano and singing. Music is not a side note to my research; it is one of the main reasons I care about building expressive, controllable, and human-facing audio AI systems.

Research Focus

My work is organized around three connected threads: controllable voice generation, music and audio intelligence, and multimodal human signal generation.

Speech and Singing Voice Generation

Speech synthesis, singing voice synthesis, voice conversion, technique control, and expressive vocal modeling.

Music and Audio Intelligence

Neural audio codecs, controllable audio generation, symbolic music generation, and creative machine learning systems.

Audio-Driven Human Generation

Talking head generation, co-speech gesture generation, affective speech, and multimodal human-centered AI.
